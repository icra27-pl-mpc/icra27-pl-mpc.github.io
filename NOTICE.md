# Website attribution

The website presentation adapts the earlier PL-MPC page and the
[Nerfies template](https://github.com/nerfies/nerfies.github.io).
The current page adds a cream, plum, and amber palette, local Outfit / DM Sans
fonts, a film-first opening, editorial section layouts, and interactive results.

The website presentation is provided under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Changes include new PL-MPC content, diagrams, media, data, component tabs,
paired video controls, progressive enhancement, and accessibility refinements.

The manuscript, figures, videos, and scientific results remain the work of
their respective research authors. The template license does not grant
additional rights to the research materials.

Outfit and DM Sans use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their licenses are
included in `static/fonts/`.

This is an independent research project page and an anonymous ICRA 2027
submission, not an official conference website or an announcement of acceptance.
